How to say "I miss you" in Chinese — 12 ways

The everyday phrase is 我想你 (wǒ xiǎng nǐ). Here's how to make it softer, stronger, sweeter — or say it in numbers.

#ChinesePinyinMeaning & when to use
1我想你wǒ xiǎng nǐI miss you — the standard, works for anyone.
2想你了xiǎng nǐ leMissing you (now) — casual, very common in texts.
3好想你hǎo xiǎng nǐI miss you so much — warm and emotional.
4我很想你wǒ hěn xiǎng nǐI really miss you — sincere, slightly formal.
5想死你了xiǎng sǐ nǐ leMiss you to death — playful exaggeration for close people.
6我想念你wǒ xiǎng niàn nǐI miss you (deeply) — more literary, for long separations.
7我思念你wǒ sī niàn nǐI long for you — poetic, romantic or for someone gone.
8我好挂念你ngo5 hou2 gwaa3 nim6 nei5Cantonese: I miss you / I worry about you — caring, family.
9我掛住你ngo5 gwaa3 zyu6 nei5Cantonese: I miss you — everyday Hong Kong usage.
10想想你xiǎng xiǎng nǐMissing you, missing you — tender, the meaning of 330.
11530wǔ sān língNumber code for 我想你.
12330330sān sān líng…想想你，想想你 — thinking of you, over and over.

Grammar notes that make you sound natural

  • 了 (le) adds "now/already": 想你了 = "I've started missing you" — perfect for the day after a visit.
  • 好 / 很 / 特别 intensify: 好想你 (so much), 特别想你 (especially much).
  • 想 also means "want / think", so context matters: 我想你 is "I miss you", but 我想吃 is "I want to eat".
  • Add a name or nickname first: 宝贝，想你了 ("Baby, missing you").

Replies when someone says they miss you

  • 我也想你 (wǒ yě xiǎng nǐ) — I miss you too.
  • 我更想你 (wǒ gèng xiǎng nǐ) — I miss you more.
  • 快回来吧 (kuài huí lái ba) — Come back soon.
  • 很快就见面了 (hěn kuài jiù jiàn miàn le) — We'll see each other soon.
