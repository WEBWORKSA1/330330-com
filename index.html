FAQ

Questions about 330330

What does 330330 mean in Chinese?

In Chinese number-codes, 3 sounds like 想 (xiǎng, "to miss / think of") and 0 stands for 你 (nǐ, "you"). 330 reads as 想想你, "missing you, missing you". Doubled, 330330 means "thinking of you, over and over". Read the full guide →

What's the difference between 520 and 530?

520 sounds like 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ, "I love you"). 530 sounds like 我想你 (wǒ xiǎng nǐ, "I miss you"). Send 520530 for both.

Are the tools really free?
