About 330330

Distance is part of modern life: study abroad, work visas, family across oceans, love that started online. 330330 exists to make missing someone a little easier.

Why "330330"?

In Chinese number-code, 3 sounds like 想 (xiǎng, "to miss") and 0 stands for 你 (nǐ, "you"). 330 is 想想你, "missing you, missing you", and doubled, 330330 means "thinking of you, over and over". It's also a reminder that 3 sounds like 生 (shēng, "life") — love that carries on, life after life.

What we build

  • Words: 100+ original miss-you messages in English and Chinese, and a dictionary of number love-codes.
  • Tools: reunion countdowns, time-zone clocks, distance calculators, e-cards and quizzes, all free and private.
  • Guides: festival calendars, gift guides and data-backed long-distance relationship advice.
  • Help: free matching with coaches, tutors, gift concierges and trip planners.
