How to choose a long-distance gift

  1. Connection beats price. Gifts that create a moment of contact — a lamp that glows, a bracelet that taps — get used daily.
  2. Time it to the festival. Order 10–14 days before Valentine's, 520 and Qixi; international shipping slows down near holidays.
  3. Think about their culture. Avoid clocks (送钟 sounds like attending a funeral) and sets of four for Chinese recipients; red and gold wrapping is always welcome.
  4. Add words. A gift plus a handwritten line or e-card is remembered far longer.
